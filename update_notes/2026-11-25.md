# Update notes – 2026-11-25

GUI only. No firmware upload needed.

## Torque and ON/OFF

- Torque 0% switches a motor OFF; above 0 switches it ON. Switching ON by hand at 0% restores its last torque. Switching OFF by hand keeps the bar value.
- Green = ON, red = OFF on the switch (track and knob), its text and the row caption. Grey = not fitted.
- Create Shape switches hand-OFF motors (bar above 0) back ON, except rows the waiting item plans at torque 0.
- Stop Animation restores each motor's torque and ON/OFF state from before the animation.

## Agents

- `set_joint_settings` (Designer): torque / stiffness only, applied instantly.
- `adjust_current_animation` (Motion): speed, swing, torque, stiffness of the playing animation, instantly (the waiting one only if nothing plays).
- Agents see which motors you switched OFF and keep them at 0 unless the request needs them ON.
- Best of 2: two Designer requests in parallel, the better one kept. `"designer_candidates"`: 2 or 1.
- New Chat resends a loaded file's parameters and the current shape / animation's description.
- A torque / stiffness list that isn't 11 numbers is ignored with a notice.

## Ghost preview

- Red "FREE" block: will be torque 0, drawn at the real motor's angle.
- Yellow "ON" block: OFF now, will be ON when the item starts.
- The READY note lists both.

## Voice input

- Hold the mic button (beside Send) and speak; the words go into the chat box.
- Uses OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` (~$0.003/min). Key in `gui/Gui_3_0/openai_key.txt` (git-ignored) or `OPENAI_API_KEY`.
- Optional `"voice_model"` and `"voice_language"` (default `"en"`) in `local_config.json`.
- Code: `Voice.pde`.
