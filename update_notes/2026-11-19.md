# Update notes – 2026-11-19

GUI only. No firmware upload needed.

## Mode names

- "AI mode" → **Automation mode**; "manipulation mode" → **Manipulation mode**. This applies to all GUI text, comments and config comments.
- The switch reads **AUTOMATION | MANIPULATION**.
- Renamed: `MODE_AI_COLOR` → `MODE_AUTOMATION_COLOR`, `AI_FOLLOW_HAND_MOVES` → `AUTOMATION_FOLLOW_HAND_MOVES`.

## Motor bars

- Bars are 210 wide (was 170). The selector, the ON/OFF switch and the left panel moved or widened to fit.
- The white load line is thicker (4 px + dark outline) with small caps top and bottom.
- **Load text under every bar**, always, e.g. "load 42% of full torque". It is white, yellow from 75%, and red from the 95% overload trip. It shows "(OFF)" for limp rows and "–" in Manipulation mode or offline. It replaces the old "now NN%" on the bar.

## Load colors on the 3D blocks

- Each motor block is colored by its live load: blue (0%) → green → yellow → **red (100% of full torque)**. A color key shows top-right.
- **LOAD COLORS (3D)** switch, top-right of the 3D view: click to turn it on/off at any time. `"load_colors_3d"` in `local_config.json` sets how it starts (default on).
- Only in Automation mode with live loads. Manipulation mode / offline use the normal colors.

## AI agents: real arm geometry

- Both agents' prompts now start with the arm's real build:
  - 12 XL330 motors, 34 mm long, 26 × 20 mm cross-section, 8 mm gaps → 42 mm between joints, ~50 cm in total.
  - **Nothing is mounted**: both ends are free. Motor 1 is unpowered but not fixed; motor 2's joint swings it like any other link.
  - The 11 joints are motors 2–12. "Base" = the motor-1 end, used only as the reference end.
  - Alternating bend planes (odd/even joints), the ±90° limit, and real feature sizes (link ~4 cm, visible curve ~8–13 cm, full-arm circle ~16 cm).
- Every "fixed base / mount" wording in the prompts, the tool descriptions and the Critic now says "base end (reference)".
- `AI_ARM_GEOMETRY_NOTE` in `Gui_3_0.pde`, built from the 3D-geometry constants (`MOTOR_*_MM`).
